# Assets and third-party notices

The modern website reuses CodeIN's original logo, hero illustration, event posters, social artwork, and historical website screenshot. Their original files and any accompanying notices are preserved unchanged under `legacy/website/assets`; the archive inventory records their SHA-256 hashes. These historical assets remain attributed to their original community context.

The locally hosted Arvo and Open Sans font packages are distributed with their original notices under `docs/licenses/`. The browser ZIP implementation uses the MIT-licensed `fflate` package. Next.js, React, Tailwind, and other dependencies retain the licenses distributed by their package authors. Consult the committed lockfile and package manifests for the exact dependencies in this release.

Contributor avatars are requested directly from GitHub's public avatar service. Profile links point to GitHub. No unrelated stock photographs or generated people are used as community members.
